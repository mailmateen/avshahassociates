Practice Areas & Compliance Portfolio

End-to-End Corporate Legal, ROC, SEBI & FEMA Practice Disciplines

Incorporation

Company & LLP Formation

End-to-end incorporation under SPICe+ for Private Limited, Section 8, Public Companies, and LLPs, including name reservation, MOA/AOA drafting, and PAN/TAN.

Section 204

Secretarial Audit (MR-3)

Statutory secretarial audits for listed corporations and eligible public entities under the Companies Act 2013, ensuring rigorous verification of governance practices.

ROC & MCA

MCA Annual & Event Filings

Punctual submission of AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC, DPT-3 deposits, plus event-driven changes in share capital, directors, and registered offices.

Listed Entities

SEBI (LODR) & Insider Trading

Advisory on Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR), SDD compliance under Prohibition of Insider Trading, and stock exchange submissions.

Cross-Border

FEMA & RBI FIRMS Reporting

Advisory on inbound Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), single master form reporting on the RBI FIRMS portal (FC-GPR / FC-TRS), and annual FLA returns.

Tribunal

NCLT & Corporate Restructuring

Section 233 fast-track mergers, capital reduction schemes, revival of struck-off companies (Sec 252), and statutory compounding before NCLT benches.

Intellectual Property

Trademark & IPR Registration

Comprehensive trademark availability search, application filings, examination report replies, objection hearings, and IPR licensing agreements.

Legal Drafting

Shareholder Agreements & ESOPs

Entity Exit
